About

I make digital images in two dimensions and three. This page collects twelve of them. Each is drawn from code in your browser instead of loaded as a picture, so you can watch it move and roll a new version.

  • 2DCanvas 2D, noise fields, blend modes and pixel buffers.
  • 3DThree.js scenes, instancing, vertex displacement and lighting.
  • VariationEvery piece runs from a seed. Reroll it in the viewer for a new result.
